Service Design

Trusted Contact Program

A safety net for members. Backup for the fraud team.

A new service that helps Golden 1 spot and stop financial exploitation. It fits the teams and tools already in place.

Role
Sr Service Designer
Partners
Fraud, IT, Legal, Compliance, Digital, front-line staff
Methods
Research, capability mapping, interviews, blueprinting
Timeline
May – July 2025

Problem

When internal teams suspect fraud, it's hard to get ahold of members and convince them of what's happening.

Solution

A Trusted Contact program that provides Golden 1 teams with contact details of a member's selected support system.

01 · Context

Contextual research to inform system design.

I studied the legal, competitive and psychological landscape of fraud to show why this is important and how to best craft the process.

Competitors

Legal

California law proposals to require trusted contact programs along a nationwide push for legislation against scams

02 · Research

Internal interviews with the fraud team to map current processes and friction points.

I wanted to understand what pain points our fraud team experiences and ideate on how a system like this could best support them. 

Fraud team interviews Stakeholder interviews Competitive analysis Capability mapping

03 · Insights

Additional safeguards

Groomed members don’t believe it’s a scam and staff can’t convince them on their own.

Speed bumps buy time

Every fraud case is time sensitive. A buffer would allow them a bit more time to investigate.

Protect the vulnerable

Current interventions are bandaid solutions as the same victims are often targeted again.

Shared empowerment

Fraud teams feel like they're the only ones helping members and they feel defeated by the lack of support.

“It’s frustrating because we’re the only ones helping our members. Everything is on our shoulders.”
Fraud team member

04 · SYSTEM DESIGN

Integrating with existing tools and processes.

Build into existing workflows

Why Limited resources meant the first iteration has to work with what we have. Additionally, it's hard for an overloaded team to absorb a new system.

Design for all three channels, launch in branches first

Why Implement, test, iterate. Once the we've worked out the kinks, we can expand across all channels.

05 · COMMUNICATION PLAN

Outlining when to call a trusted contact without breaking their current flow.

The blueprint (top of page) maps all four channels, then zooms in on branches. With the fraud team, I built a communication plan that fits their current procedures.
