Product Design

EltaAI

A safety app, from prototype to App Store.

EltaAI alerts your people when an outing goes wrong. I led research, the PRD and design, and rebuilt the core flows.

Role
Lead UX Designer & Strategist
Team
Founder, AI engineering, designers
Methods
Audit, interviews, survey, testing
Timeline
May – October 2025

Challenge

Experienced explorers trust their instincts, at times overlooking risks, skipping preparation, and failing to alert others of their whereabouts.

Solution

An app that uses AI to build each trip’s checklist from weather, history and alerts, It shares the plan with a user's safety network to keep everyone in the loop.

01 · Audit

UX audit of the founder's build to refine IA

I mapped every screen and the relationship between objects, marking what to combine, move or cut. Trip creation became the center.

Major observations

Contacts and activities belong in the plan. Onboarding was missing.

Areas of focus

A shorter plan flow, clear contacts, a checklist, onboarding.

Open questions

Is “trip” too narrow? Is it the solution users are looking for?

02 · Scope

Sketching through contexts with founder.

These sessions led to larger research questions: What are the escalation paths after missing a "check-in?" What information needs to be communicated to safety contacts? How do we maintain the users privacy and trust?

03 · LITERATURE REVIEW

Understanding outdoor safety systems.

Literature exposed me to work that uses sensors to pick up on environmental and biological data in real time to provide detailed alerts to users about their location.

 Although we don't have the resources to plant sensors around the globe, we could combine location tracking, past and present weather patterns, and relevant online activity (community and government alerts), to support their preparedness.

PRIMARY RESEARCH QUESTION

How might we design a communication flow that provides safety while also maintaining privacy?

Secondary finding · 01

Participants rely on direct communication, like texts or calls, rather than tech like location tracking.

Secondary finding · 02

People forget to follow up, and the responsibility shifts onto the person being alerted.

Interview insights

We interviewed 11 participants, using the survey to shape our questions. While the other two designers explored safety and the outdoors, I probed safety and communication.

EltaAI could stand out by helping people plan, share those plans with loved ones, and use AI to keep everyone in a continuous loop of communication.

  • People want loved ones to know their plans and how long they’ll be gone, especially when they can’t be reached.

    CommunicationSafety6 of 11

  • Keeping others updated feels like pressure. In the flow of the day, people forget to follow up.

    ADHDCommunication5 of 11

  • On risky trips, people share practical details but hold some back to avoid causing worry.

    CommunicationTravel5 of 11

  • People want help preparing: trail conditions, food and water for long hikes, gear for multi-day trips.

    PackingPreparedness4 of 11

07 · Shipped Product

Phase 1 Features

Quick Timer

A quick way to let a safety contact know you’re out.

We shipped this as a test. The MVP centered on long outdoor trips, but we also wanted to explore check-ins for a different group: people on quick outings who want easy reassurance. After launch, usage would tell us whether it’s worth building out.

Long Trip Plan

For people heading out on adventures, and the people thinking of them.

Trips combines location, past and current weather, and relevant online activity to flag conditions before users head out. It also shares the itinerary and expected return time with their contacts automatically, so no one has to keep checking in.

Adding Activities & AI Generated Checklist

Pick activities. No times needed. They feed an AI-generated checklist that people can keep, edit or add to in one pass.

Ask Ellie – AI Chat

Ellie helps users with questions about their trips and helps them tailor their plans when needed.
