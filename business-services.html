Service Design

Business Services

Finding the friction before a new service launch.

Business Services was about to launch. Leadership needed to know how opening an account felt, online and in branch. I audited the journey and made the friction hard to ignore.

Role
Lead Service Designer & UX Researcher
Partners
Business Services, branch reps, executive leadership
Methods
Interviews, heuristic analysis, benchmarking, contextual inquiry, storyboards, blueprints
Timeline
April 2025 – February 2026

Problem

Business account opening was a new and major strategic initiative for Golden 1. Despite its magnitude, both the online and in-branch processes were hard to complete.

Solution

A redesigned online account opening process and recommendations to increase efficiencies in branch, relayed via service blueprint and storyboards.

04 · Storyboards

Allowing stakeholders to connect to design needs through stories.

I made two short storyboards: one from a member’s view, one celebrating what worked. The CEO felt the frustration and asked for the fixes before launch.

High priority

  • Add a back button
  • Flag important information up front
  • Update titles and show detailed progress

Lower priority

  • Explain NAICS codes
  • Signal required fields
  • Rearrange similar questions

05 · SERVICE DESIGN

Addressing in-branch inefficiencies.

Through interviews with Business Account Reps, contextual inquiry, and log data analysis, I developed an understanding of the current end-to-end experience along with its inefficiencies.

Problem statement

How might we streamline in-branch account opening to reduce the average 2-hour completion time?

2 hrs

average to open a business account in branch

~10 min

lost to reps switching between four systems

6

friction points, each paired with a fix

  1. 01Before the visit

    Challenge: Members arrive without an appointment or documents

    Opportunity: Required docs shown at booking, plus reminder emails

  2. 02Turned away

    Challenge: No consistent rep follow-up

    Opportunity: Rep outreach added to the procedure

  3. 03Funding

    Challenge: Account linking fails; members re-send statements

    Opportunity: Pause linking; drop statements for existing members

  4. 04Missing documents

    Challenge: Back to the branch to reschedule

    Opportunity: Resume by phone or video call

  5. 05Remote ID

    Challenge: Photo capture glitches lose applicants

    Opportunity: Photo upload plus a one-time passcode

  6. 06Rep workstation

    Challenge: Four systems on one screen, about 10 extra minutes

    Opportunity: A second monitor
